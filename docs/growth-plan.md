# 1 Oak Dental: organic growth plan

What is already built into the site (as of 2026-11-25): one domain per office, service page per treatment with FAQ and MedicalProcedure schema, Dentist schema with hours, neighbourhoods and Google profile link, breadcrumbs, related-service links, sitemap, robots, OG image, photo and video slots, click-to-load video, no tracking scripts, static pages (fast by default).

## Before launch (one-time)
1. Set hours and website on the Google Business Profile (done: hours; todo: website).
2. Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 to 1oakdental.ca; submit the sitemap.
3. Put the same name, address and phone (NAP) everywhere: Google, site, Instagram, LinkedIn, RCDSO listing, insurance directories. Phone is (416) 214-0003.
4. Replace line drawings with real photos; add the first video (All-on-4).
5. Add the booking link (Akitu One) so every button books directly.

## First 90 days (highest value first)
1. **Google reviews.** Target 30 reviews in 90 days. Ask every happy patient at checkout with the short review link from the Google profile. Reply to every review. This outranks anything on the website for "dentist near me".
2. **Google Business Profile posts.** One post a week: a service, a before/after, a team note. Add 10+ photos of the office and team. Enable messaging.
3. **Referring dentists.** Email every referring office the /referring-dentists/ link and the pad PDFs. Ask three friendly offices to link to 1oakdental.ca from their "specialists we work with" page. Local links from real dental offices are the strongest links a practice can get.
4. **Directories.** Make sure the office is listed, with matching NAP, on: Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, RateMDs, Opencare, Medimap, 123dentist-style directories, ODA/RCDSO find-a-dentist, Sun Life Lumino and insurer provider lists.
5. **Search Console check.** At 30 and 90 days: which queries show the site, which pages get impressions but few clicks (rewrite titles), any crawl errors.

## Content calendar (after the first 90 days)
One article a month, each answering a real question patients ask, with Dr. Taimish as the author:
- Wisdom teeth recovery day by day
- All-on-4 vs dentures: an honest comparison
- What sedation feels like, and who should have it
- Dental implant cost in Toronto: what changes the price (ranges, no exact figures)
- First dental visit for a toddler: what happens
- Invisalign for adults: how long and what it is like
Each article links to its service page and back. Rebuild the two or three old 416dentist.com posts that had traffic (needs Search Console data from the old site).

## Later
- Neighbourhood pages only if Search Console shows searches like "dentist Corktown" or "dentist Regent Park" with no page answering them. Each one must say something specific (transit, parking, nearby landmarks), not be a copy of the homepage.
- Before/after gallery with consent; a second video per quarter.
- Once reviews pass ~50, feature the rating in the homepage strip (already wired in `practice.reviewCount`).
- Revisit the 416dentist.com redirect six weeks after launch.
